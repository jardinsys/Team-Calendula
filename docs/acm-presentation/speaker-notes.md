## 4 · Scale + neglect (1:00)
- **1 billion+** live with a mental health condition (WHO, Sept 2025); care reaches only a fraction
- Neglect is uneven — and measurable (IAMHRF 2020, 75k+ grants): **<2%** of funding targets conditions causing **>50%** of mental-illness deaths · **95%** of funding in high-income countries
- [Counter runs 1.4s]

## 5 · AI: the evidence (0:55) ← follows the scale slide
- Hands: "know someone who talks to ChatGPT about things they'd tell a therapist?"
- Gradient: **1 in 4** adults (surveys 2025, 22–24%) → **1 in 2** with ongoing conditions (Rousmaniere, 2025, 49%) → **4 in 5** in treatment never told their therapist (clinical sample, 2026, 83%)
- Quote: JeeP, 60 — "prepare for it… much more clarity" (npj Mental Health Research 2024)
- "Nobody's first choice… it's the option that was actually available. The use of AI is the trace evidence of the gap."
- Risk boxes: OpenAI self-reported **1M+/week** suicide-planning indicators · ACM FAcT 2025 (Stanford): stigma + no suicidal-intent interruption
- ⚠ Most users were already in care — "the people with the fewest alternatives lean hardest"

## 6 · Technology gap (1:10) ← follows AI; bridge: "So why wasn't it built?"
- Feasible now: platforms, real-time collab, AI, privacy engineering
- Research funding fell back to **2014 levels** by 2023 (IAMHRF)
- Governments: median **2%** of health budgets (WHO Atlas)
- Investment concentrates in **generic formats** (teletherapy · meditation · mood trackers) — and now generic AI chatbots (callback to slide 5)
- On-slide quote, let it land: "…rather than a lack of need" (npj Digital Medicine — why AI investment skips mental health)
- The reach exists (meta-analysis, 48 studies · 10,324 people, npj Digital Medicine 2025): internet use **87%** — near-parity with the general population; most want mobile support (Firth 2016 concurs)
- Punch: "The need is there. The capability is there. **The investment is where?**" + asterisk: PTSD = the standing exception (government-funded, next slide)

## 7 · PTSD: proof the model works (0:45)
- The exception that proves the rule: sustained **public** investment → purpose-built software
- **01** VA app suite (free, government-built) · **02** VR exposure therapy in clinical use · **03** 170+ clinical sites, most VA medical centers
- The catch: concentrated at the VA — for veterans; the other trauma-based conditions, and most people living with them, are on the outside
- "There is proof of the model… who gets to benefit is the open question"
- Sources: VA National Center for PTSD (app suite) · Rizzo et al., USC Institute for Creative Technologies (BRAVEMIND) — 170+ clinical sites, largest RCT equivalent to prolonged exposure (Difede/Rothbaum/Rizzo 2022)
