## Framing constraints (non-negotiable, apply to ALL presentation content)

1. **Clinical vocabulary only.** DSM/ICD-style terms (dissociative disorders, identity states, transitions, functioning, privacy). No community terminology: never "plurality", "alters", "fronting", "switching" in public-facing/academic work.
2. **Never equate "plurality" with dissociative disorders.** The word "plurality" itself is avoided entirely in Jardin's academic and product-facing work.
3. **Never cite ISSTD.** Use APA DSM-5-TR, MSD Manuals, or peer-reviewed literature for dissociative-disorder claims. Peer-reviewed anchor for prevalence: Johnson, Cohen, Kasen, & Brook (2006), *Journal of Psychiatric Research* (1.5% in a 658-adult community sample).
4. **Honest build status.** Systemiser is "being built," prototype testing has exercised 4 core cycles. NEVER claim a working product / shipped product. Grounding + crisis tools are roadmap items ("under active development"), not tested.
5. **Continuity framing.** Systemiser's unifying pitch: restoring continuity of memory, identity, and the organization of daily life when dissociative symptoms disrupt it. Presentation should carry this framing, not just list features.
6. **Ecosystem framing.** Systemiser = standalone application ecosystem (desktop, mobile, web) with planned gateways into third-party platform categories (messaging/collaboration e.g. Discord and Slack; in-vehicle e.g. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto). Discord is ONE gateway, not the platform.
